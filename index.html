How the numbers are computed

Recovery (Est.) = 50% sleep score + 30% HRV component + 20% Body Battery charged. HRV component = 50 + (last night's HRV − your weekly average HRV) × 2, clamped to 0–100.

Strain (Est.) = min(21, HR load + min(5, steps ÷ 2000) + min(6, strength minutes ÷ 15)), rounded. HR load = min(15, sum(max(0, HR − resting HR) × minutes) ÷ 3000) over full-day samples. Strength minutes = strength training only.

Rankings: higher wins, #1 is green. Overall strip: 3/2/1 points per ranked board, ties share, max 12. The points race below the photos shows each total out of 12.

~ = partial day. — = nothing recorded, not zero. Weekly averages use recorded days only. Anything (Est.) is modeled, not a Garmin or Whoop score.
