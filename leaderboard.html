How the numbers are computed

Recovery (Est.) = 50% sleep score + 30% HRV component + 20% Body Battery charged. HRV component = 50 + (last night's HRV − your own weekly average HRV) × 2, clamped to 0–100. A modeled estimate, not a Garmin score.

Strain (Est.) = min(21, max(0, steps ÷ 1000 + max(0, max HR − resting HR − 40) ÷ 10 + min(6, strength minutes ÷ 15))), rounded to the nearest whole number. Steps capture volume, peak heart rate captures cardio load (40 bpm of spread counts as no real exertion peak), and strength minutes capture lifting load that steps and heart rate miss. Only strength-training activities count toward the third term, so cardio is not double-counted. The ~ means the day is still partial. Not a Garmin or Whoop number.

Sleep score is Garmin's own 0–100 score for the night ending that morning. Not calculated here.

HRV is Garmin's last-night average HRV in milliseconds. The line under Recovery compares it to your own 7-day average only. HRV is personal and is never ranked across people.

Resting HR is Garmin's daily resting heart rate in bpm. The weekly board ranks each person's lowest value of the week; lower wins.

Steps and distance are Garmin's daily totals; distance is in miles.

Body Battery values are Garmin's. Only the charged value feeds Recovery.

Rankings: higher wins, except Resting HR where lower wins. The #1 value is green.

Expanded rows show context for that metric only. Recovery expands to its three inputs, Steps expands to distance. Distance never appears under Resting HR.

Missing data: — means nothing was recorded that day (Jordan has no sleep or HRV for Oct 23), not zero. Weekly averages use recorded days only.

Daily vs Weekly: Daily defaults to the last full day (Oct 24). Today (Oct 25) is partial, as of ~7:35 PM PDT. Weekly covers Oct 19–25.
